Overview

What this project is

An active aerodynamic front wing system designed for a Formula SAE car — full scale, fully servo-actuated, with two functional modes: a low-drag DRS position for straight-line speed and a high-downforce cornering position for grip.

The wing is designed from scratch in Fusion 360, optimized through ANSYS Fluent CFD, then physically fabricated using composite layups and 3D-printed tooling. The servo mechanism is integrated directly into the endplate assembly, controlled via a microcontroller with CAN-BUS communication.

This build guide covers every phase: concept, CAD, CFD analysis, mold fabrication, composite layup, actuation hardware, and electronics integration. Everything a small team would need to build and validate their own system.

Build Process

Phase by phase

01
Phase 1 — Concept & Research Done
Aero targets, rule compliance, and baseline geometry
  • Defined downforce and drag targets from team lap sim data
  • Reviewed FSAE rulebook constraints (max chord, overhang limits, structural req.)
  • Benchmarked existing FSAE wing designs and published aero papers
  • Selected airfoil profile: NACA 4412 modified for ground effect
02
Phase 2 — CAD Design Done
Full parametric model in Fusion 360
  • Wing body, endplates, and mount brackets modeled parametrically
  • Servo horn and linkage geometry designed for target actuation range
  • Endplate integrated servo pocket — no external housing
  • Mold split lines defined; master mold designed for 3D printing in two halves
03
Phase 3 — CFD Validation Done
ANSYS Fluent simulation — two actuation states
  • Steady-state k-ω SST turbulence model at 60 km/h freestream
  • Ground plane modeled at static ride height (35 mm)
  • Compared Cl and Cd between DRS (0°) and high-DF (14°) positions
  • Pressure and velocity contour maps extracted for guide documentation
04
Phase 4 — Mold & Fabrication In Progress
3D-printed mold tooling and composite layup
  • Master mold printed in PLA+ at 0.15 mm layer height, sanded to Ra < 1.6 µm
  • Three-layer GFRP wet layup with 45/0/45 ply schedule
  • Vacuum bagged at 23 inHg for 12 h cure
  • Post-cure trimming, drilling, and insert installation
05
Phase 5 — Actuation & Electronics Upcoming
Servo installation, control board, and CAN integration
  • MG996R servos mounted in endplate pockets
  • Arduino Nano as local controller, reading CAN-BUS signals from ECU
  • DRS trigger: throttle > 95% AND straight-line detection (IMU yaw rate)
  • Full wiring harness routed along front crash structure
06
Phase 6 — On-Car Validation Upcoming
Testing, data logging, and iteration
  • Skid-pad and endurance run with DRS active/inactive logging
  • Load cell on mount bracket to measure actual aerodynamic loads
  • Lap time delta compared to fixed-wing configuration
