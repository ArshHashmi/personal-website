Span check: 4 x 200 + 112 + 250 + 247 = 1,409 mm of parts, which assembles to
1,400 mm once the dowelled joints close up. The summed solid volume of those
seven pieces is within 0.6% of the one-piece reference model, so the set is
complete.

Print settings: PLA, 5% infill, 3 walls, 0.2 mm layers.
The connecting arm is the exception: CF-PLA and 4 walls. It is the part that
fractured on the original build.
