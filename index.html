I'm Arsh, a Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science student at Binghamton chasing a career in motorsport.

I've always liked taking things apart to understand how they work, then trying to build something better. That's what pulled me toward engineering in the first place, and it's the same thing pushing me toward motorsport now. Most of my time goes into the shop or a CAD screen, working through builds from a rough idea to something that actually runs. I've also picked up a few things along the way that don't fit neatly into "engineer": I trade and backtest strategies in Python, and I ran my own business for a few years before college. Right now I'm looking for motorsport, automotive, or R&D internships where I can keep building.

Perfect for pricing logic, no-arbitrage arguments, discounted cash flow setups, and any problem where showing the reasoning matters as much as the final number.
