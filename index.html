I'm a Mechanical Engineering + Computer Science student at Binghamton University on a direct path into motorsport engineering — designing, building, and testing the systems that make vehicles faster, smarter, and more precise.

My hands-on experience spans aerodynamic design, CAD/CFD simulation, fabrication, and on-car testing. I've designed and validated active aerodynamic components in ANSYS and Fusion 360, contributed to EV and autonomous vehicle projects, and worked through complete build cycles from concept to bench test. I also took 2nd place nationally at the Michigan Investment Group Quant Conference — a credential that sharpened my analytical edge under pressure, competing against teams from MIT, Stanford, and UCB.

Beyond the build, I design and backtest algorithmic trading strategies in Python, hold certifications from UPenn, Duke, and Bloomberg, and have run my own entrepreneurial venture from the ground up — which means I know how to ship things without a safety net.

I'm currently seeking engineering internships in motorsport, automotive, or advanced R&D — and always open to connecting with engineers, teams, and recruiters pushing the edge of what vehicles can do.
