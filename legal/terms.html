Using the files.

Last updated 3 September 2026

The short version

Build one for yourself. Do not hand the files on, and do not sell them or anything made from them. Everything below is that sentence in more detail.

What you may do

  • Download the files and print them for your own personal, non-commercial use.
  • Build one. That is what the guide is for.
  • Modify the parts for your own build, your own car, your own printer.
  • Show what you built. Post it, film it, write about it. A credit and a link back is appreciated but not demanded.

What you may not do

  • Redistribute the files. Do not re-upload them to model repositories, file lockers, Discord servers or anywhere else. Link to this page instead, so anyone who wants them gets the current version and the context around it.
  • Sell them, or sell anything printed from them. No selling the files, no selling printed parts, no selling finished wings, no bundling them into a paid product or course.
  • Pass the design off as your own, or publish a derivative of these files as if you had drawn them.
  • Use them commercially in any other form without asking me first. Ask, and the answer may well be yes.

These files are shared so people can learn from them and build one. That only keeps working if they stay in one place with their context attached, which is the whole reason for the redistribution rule.
